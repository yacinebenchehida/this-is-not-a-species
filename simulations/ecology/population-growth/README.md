# The Petri Dish

[Français](README.fr.md) · [Play the animation](play.html) · [Cell division up close](motion.html)

Watch nutrients enter cells, help them grow and enable division. One dish receives a continuous nutrient supply; the other uses up a finite reserve and gradually slows down. Golden angular parcels represent nutrients, and the colourful characters represent bacteria.

Change the starting bacteria, potential division time or nutrient supply. Supply is described as None, Small, Medium, Large or Abundant. With no nutrients, the limited cells wait until you supply some. “Add nutrients” replenishes the existing limited population without resetting it, so you can see growth pick up again. The graph records each addition; “Inspect the slowdown” gives a closer view of the limited population.

Every character is one whole cell. Both dishes share a drawing scale that decreases to keep all cells visible. Shapes and colours are decorative. Fast playback preserves every division count and the same resource rules. Reduced motion retains static nutrient-reserve illustrations, counts and curves while suppressing travelling parcels and stretching.

Play, pause, advance ten model minutes, reset, or change playback speed. Changing starting conditions restarts both populations; adding nutrients preserves them. The first view pauses after four model hours or at 256 cells in the replenished dish. Adding nutrients can continue the story, up to eight model hours or 512 cells. The end of a viewing window is not a biological stop to growth.

The optional explanation includes exponential and logistic equations and their assumptions. Nutrient use is explicitly coupled to growth, but the parcels and their paths are schematic representations rather than independently simulated molecules. This is an educational illustration, not a particular bacterial species or a complete laboratory culture. See [MODEL.md](MODEL.md) for the stated rules and checks.
